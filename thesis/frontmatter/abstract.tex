\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
This thesis studies the construction and numerical validation of static quark--antiquark trial states based on low-lying eigenmodes of the three-dimensional gauge-covariant lattice Laplace operator. The central long-term goal is to use these sources to measure gluonic flux-tube profiles through local action-density and energy-density insertions in lattice QCD.

The first stage validates the Laplacian-eigenmode source construction by computing static correlators \Ls, extracting effective energies \Veff, and benchmarking the resulting static potential against the expected Cornell form. The present implementation uses static perambulators built from Laplacian eigenvectors and temporal Wilson lines, averages over several temporal source positions, and applies correlated plateau and potential fits. The current numerical status is a validated production pipeline for \(R/a=1,\ldots,6\) with a stable plateau window around \(\tau/a=3,4,5\), together with diagnostic scans at larger separations.

The second stage, to be completed as the main physics result of the thesis, introduces local gluonic probes based on plaquette or clover estimators of \(E^2\) and \(B^2\). The normalized, vacuum-subtracted ratio of the source-probe correlator to the source correlator will then provide the spatial modification of the gluonic action density induced by the static \QQbar\ pair.
