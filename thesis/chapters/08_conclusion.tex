\chapter{Conclusions and outlook}
\label{chap:conclusion}

The present thesis draft starts from a validated Laplace-static source pipeline. The key benchmark result is that Laplacian-eigenmode static correlators produce stable effective-energy plateaus for \(R/a=1,\ldots,6\), and the resulting potential is described by a Cornell form with acceptable correlated fit quality. This confirms that the source construction is sufficiently mature to normalize local gluonic probe measurements.

The remaining core physics task is to insert local action-density and energy-density probes into the Laplace-static correlator and extract the spatial gluonic response of the gauge field around a static \QQbar\ pair. Once this stage is completed, the thesis will connect the static-potential validation with the final flux-tube profiles and discuss the limitations from statistics, operator overlap, probe discretization, and fit-window systematics.
